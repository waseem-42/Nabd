## Exercise illustrations

The exercise illustrations in `public/art/` come from **Workout Guide** by bryllim
(<https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide>), which builds on **Everkinetic**, as shipped in
GymMane. They are licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0** and remain under that license here; the files were
converted to JSON and their coordinates rounded.
The technical display treatment preserves these source poses and their CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution.

The continuous skeletal animations in `src/motion/` are original Nabd code, separate from those
source illustrations. Their movement references and scope are listed in
`docs/CONTINUOUS-BATCH-01.md`.
